01 — Fit

Who can join

Start with eligibility.

DECA’s high school program is for students in grades 9–12. New Jersey adds a course requirement: members must currently be or previously have been enrolled in an eligible business or marketing-related course.

01

Eligible coursework

Marketing, sales, service, hospitality, business finance, management, or entrepreneurship courses qualify under the current NJ DECA guidance.

02

Course note

Personal Financial Literacy courses do not qualify as the corresponding course under the current New Jersey association profile.

03

Chapter connection

Membership is registered through the school chapter. Students do not sign up independently through this website.

03 — Time

What participation involves

Plan for the work between the wins.

PHS has not published a fixed weekly schedule. Your time commitment depends on the event you choose and whether you attend conferences, so ask for the current chapter calendar before committing.

01

Meetings

Attend the local chapter meetings the advisor announces and use them to choose events, practice, and coordinate chapter work.

02

Preparation

Role-plays need repeated speaking practice; prepared events need time for research, writing, and presentation; simulations need scheduled decisions.

03

Conferences

Competition and conference participation can add travel time and associated costs. The advisor confirms which events and trips are available.

04 — Register

How the process works

Take the advisor-led route.

Email starts the conversation; the advisor confirms the chapter’s registration and payment steps. Sending a message does not complete membership.

  1. 01

    Check your course eligibility.

    Confirm that your current or previous coursework matches the New Jersey requirement.

  2. 02

    Ask the advisor for the PHS process.

    Request the local registration instructions, current chapter meeting date, payment directions, and any school forms.

  3. 03

    Complete the chapter’s official registration.

    The chapter advisor submits members through the official membership system and confirms when dues are received.

  4. 04

    Choose a starting event.

    Use the competition guide to compare formats, then ask which events PHS is entering this year.
