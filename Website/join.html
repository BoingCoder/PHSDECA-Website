Who can join

Students in grades 9 through 12 can join DECA. You need to be taking, or have already taken, an eligible business elective.

01

Eligible coursework

Any business elective other than Personal Financial Literacy can count toward the course requirement.

02

Personal Financial Literacy

A Personal Financial Literacy course does not meet NJ DECA’s current course requirement for membership.

03

Registration

You’ll register through our school chapter. Contact our officers to get started.

Membership dues

NJ DECA currently lists annual student dues at $30. Before paying, ask our officers how to submit your payment and whether conferences or travel will cost extra.

Time commitment

You can give DECA 1 hour or 100 hours. The more effort you put in, the better your chances of placing well. Personally, I’ve never felt like DECA left me short on time.

01

Meetings

Come to chapter meetings to choose your event, get some practice, and help with chapter activities.

02

Preparation

Your preparation will depend on your event. Role-plays take speaking practice. Prepared events involve research, writing, and presenting, while simulations require decisions at scheduled times.

03

Conferences

Interested in attending a competition or conference? Ask our officers which trips are available and plan for any extra travel time and costs.

How to register

Email our officers to ask about registration and payment. You’ll still need to complete those steps to become a member.

  1. 01

    Check your course eligibility.

    Check that a course you’re taking or have already completed meets NJ DECA’s requirement.

  2. 02

    Ask our officers how to register.

    Ask our officers how to register and pay, when the next meeting is, and which school forms you’ll need.

  3. 03

    Complete the chapter’s official registration.

    Our advisor will submit your registration through DECA’s membership system and confirm that your dues have been received.

  4. 04

    Choose a starting event.

    Explore the competition guide and choose an event that interests you.
