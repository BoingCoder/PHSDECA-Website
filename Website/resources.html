Competition formats

DECA offers role-plays and case studies, prepared events, and online simulations. Each has different requirements, so check the current official guide for the events and rules that apply to you.

FormatWhat you doBest fit
Role-plays & case studiesYou’ll get a business situation, work out a solution, and present it to a judge. Many events also include an exam on your career area.Do you enjoy thinking on your feet and explaining your solution before time runs out?
Prepared eventsYou’ll write a project or plan and prepare a presentation about it. Give yourself time to work on it, and follow your event’s requirements closely.Would you enjoy researching an idea and improving it over time?
Online simulationsTry making business decisions in an online simulation, such as the Stock Market Game or Virtual Business Challenge.Do you like trying different decisions in a simulation and seeing how they turn out?

How to prepare

Not sure where to start? Follow these steps, then ask someone for one thing you could improve.

  1. 01
    Confirm the event.

    Read your event’s current official page and make sure it’s offered in New Jersey this year.

  2. 02
    Check the requirements.

    Make a note of how many people can enter, what you need to submit, how much time you get to prepare and present, whether there’s an exam, and how you’ll be scored.

  3. 03
    Build a simple structure.

    Explain the situation, share your recommendation, and show why it makes sense. Finish by saying what you’d do next.

  4. 04
    Practice aloud.

    Practice with a timer. Explain the performance indicators in your own words and work on moving smoothly between your points.

  5. 05
    Get feedback.

    Ask an officer or teammate to listen. Use their feedback to improve one thing, then try the same scenario again.

Choosing an event

01

Like live problem-solving?

Try looking at role-plays or case studies. If you’re eligible, a first-year Principles of Business Administration event could also be a place to start.

02

Prefer building a plan?

Interested in developing a project or plan? Explore prepared projects, operations research, project management, entrepreneurship, or integrated marketing.

03

Think in systems and numbers?

Like working with numbers? Look into Finance or Personal Financial Literacy events, the Stock Market Game, or a Virtual Business Challenge that fits your interests.

04

Need a local recommendation?

Ask our officers for help finding an event that interests you and getting started with preparation.
