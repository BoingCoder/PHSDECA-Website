01 — Compare

Three official categories

Pick your prep style.

DECA groups high school competitive events into role-plays and case studies, prepared events, and online simulations. Exact event offerings and rules vary, so the current official guide remains the source of truth.

FormatWhat you doA good first question
Role-plays & case studiesReceive a business situation, prepare a solution, and present to a judge. Many events also include a career-cluster exam.Do I like making a clear recommendation under a time limit?
Prepared eventsDevelop a written project or plan, then deliver a prepared presentation. Start early and follow the event’s exact specifications.Do I want to research, build, and polish one idea over time?
Online simulationsMake business decisions in an online simulation such as the Stock Market Game or Virtual Business Challenge.Do I prefer repeated decisions and feedback in a digital environment?
02 — Prepare

Beginner checklist

Make the first rep count.

You do not need to know the whole event universe before you begin. Use this sequence, then ask for one specific piece of feedback.

  1. 01
    Confirm the event.

    Read the current official event page and check that the event is offered in New Jersey this year.

  2. 02
    Mark the constraints.

    Write down participants, deliverables, preparation time, presentation time, exam requirements, and evaluation criteria.

  3. 03
    Build a simple structure.

    Open with the situation, give a recommendation, support it with evidence, and close with a next step.

  4. 04
    Practice aloud.

    Use a timer, say the performance indicators in your own words, and rehearse the transition between points.

  5. 05
    Ask for one note.

    Get feedback from an advisor, officer, or teammate, change one thing, and run the same scenario again.

04 — Authorized access

DECA+

Use the chapter door.

DECA+ contains member preparation tools such as practice exams, scenarios, and other licensed materials. This site does not reproduce any of that content.

01

Ask the advisor whether PHS has an active DECA+ subscription.

DECA+ access is chapter-based. The official login requires the chapter credentials; do not create a workaround or share the login outside the chapter.

02

Open the official login only after you are authorized.

Use the chapter ID and password supplied by the advisor, then keep all exams, scenarios, and samples within the authorized chapter.
