Competitive resources · find your format

Know the room before you enter.

DECA high school events fall into three broad formats. Start here, confirm current rules with the official guide and your advisor, then practice through approved platforms.

01 — Formats
01 · Think on your feet

Role-plays & case studies

You receive a business situation on site, prepare under a time limit, and present a solution to a judge. Many events also include a career-cluster exam.

02 · Build it early

Written & prepared events

You develop a project, plan, campaign, research study, or prepared presentation before competition. Requirements vary, so start with the current official event page.

03 · Compete online

Online simulations

You manage decisions in an online business environment, such as a virtual business challenge or stock-market event, and compete through qualifying rounds.

02 — Prepare

Use the official source.

Event offerings, deadlines, and rules can change. These links go to the organizations that publish the current information. Licensed DECA+ exams and scenarios stay behind its login and are not reproduced here.

03 — Rhythm

A simple prep loop.

Read the exact guidelines. Identify what the judge evaluates. Practice once under real timing. Ask for one piece of feedback. Then run it again.

01

Understand

Confirm format, participants, deliverables, timing, exam requirements, and the current evaluation criteria.

02

Rehearse

Practice aloud, keep your structure visible, and simulate the preparation and presentation window.

03

Review

Use judge-style criteria for feedback instead of relying only on whether the presentation felt good.

04

Repeat

Change one thing at a time, protect what worked, and build confidence through deliberate repetitions.
