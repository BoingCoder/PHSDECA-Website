What PHS DECA is

PHS DECA turns business concepts into a practice space: a place to test an idea, make a case, and learn how to present it with other people.

Members explore marketing, finance, hospitality, management, entrepreneurship, and personal financial literacy through eligible coursework and New Jersey DECA competition formats.
